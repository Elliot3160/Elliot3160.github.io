About Me

Hi, I’m Elliot — a geoscience enthusiast and current Master of Science student in Palaeoclimate change at Royal Holloway. My research project is currently leveraging machine learning for Holocene tephra classification. With a background in geography and geology, I explore palaeoclimate, volcanology, and the environmental changes recorded in rocks and fossils. This website is my platform to share insights, research, and resources that connect our planet’s history to today’s challenges.
