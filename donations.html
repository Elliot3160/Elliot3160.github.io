Support Climate & Quaternary Science Outreach

I create accessible, evidence-based science communication covering tephrochronology, Quaternary environments, volcanic hazards, and past climate change. This website is designed to bridge academic research, field practice, and public understanding.

Support helps me maintain and expand educational resources, field guides, open-access explanations, and research-led tools aimed at students, early-career scientists, and anyone curious about Earth’s history.

Contributions may help cover costs associated with field activities, equipment, digital resources, web hosting, and the ongoing development of outreach-focused content. No unpublished datasets or confidential research are shared or funded through donations.
