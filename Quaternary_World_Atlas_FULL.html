How to use the atlas

Geography meets the literature

Each regional profile connects geological context with selected research papers. Search by location, discipline, theme, evidence archive, dating method or geological time interval.

Editorial standard

Curated, not just scraped

Research records should be checked for geographic relevance, Quaternary scope and bibliographic accuracy. A paper mentioning a place is not automatically evidence about that region's Quaternary history.

This is a developing collection of selected references, not a comprehensive systematic bibliography. Some metadata and classifications require further editorial verification.
