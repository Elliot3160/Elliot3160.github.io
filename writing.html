ABOUT THIS PORTFOLIO

Science deserves to be understood.

Scientific research continually advances our understanding of the natural world. However, communicating complex discoveries beyond academic journals remains an important challenge.

Through Fossils & Futures, I explore peer-reviewed scientific research and translate its findings into engaging, accessible articles for wider audiences.

My writing focuses on maintaining scientific accuracy while making complex methodologies, discoveries and environmental processes easier to understand.

Can Machine Learning Help Us Unlock Earth's Geological Past?

How artificial intelligence is changing the way scientists interpret geological records and reconstruct environmental history.

Geological records contain an extraordinary amount of information about Earth's history. From ancient volcanic eruptions to past climatic conditions, these archives help scientists reconstruct environments that existed thousands or even millions of years ago.

But interpreting these records is not always straightforward. Could machine learning provide researchers with new ways to recognise patterns, identify geological materials and unlock information hidden within complex datasets?

RESEARCH INTERESTS

Areas of Scientific Interest

My writing explores interdisciplinary research across Earth and environmental sciences, with particular interests in:

Quaternary Science

Reconstructing Earth's recent geological and environmental history through sedimentary archives and palaeoenvironmental evidence.

Palaeoclimate

Understanding past climate variability, environmental change and the processes that influence Earth's climate system.

Tephrochronology

Exploring volcanic ash deposits as chronological markers and tools for correlating geological and archaeological records.

Machine Learning

Investigating how artificial intelligence, statistical learning and computational methods can advance geological research.

Volcanology

Examining volcanic processes, explosive eruptions and the geological evidence preserved in volcanic deposits.

Environmental Change

Exploring the interactions between natural environmental processes, ecosystems and long-term climate change.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Scientific Background

I hold a First-Class BSc in Physical Geography from the University of Southampton and an MSc with Distinction in Past Climates and Environmental Change from Royal Holloway, University of London.

My academic background combines Quaternary science, palaeoclimate reconstruction, geological data analysis and machine learning. My research interests include the application of computational methods to geological datasets and the interpretation of past environmental change.

I am particularly interested in communicating scientific research beyond specialist academic audiences, helping readers understand not only what researchers have discovered, but why those discoveries matter.
