TephraLink™

High-precision tephra correlation powered by machine learning.

What is TephraLink™?

TephraLink™ is a flexible machine-learning research toolkit for tephrochronology. It provides a transparent, modular pipeline for training classifiers, applying ensemble models, working with CLR-transformed compositions, and integrating stratigraphic priors into probability outputs.

TephraLink™ is designed for exploratory research, teaching, and method development. It does not guarantee correct tephra correlations; instead, it supports researchers in building and evaluating their own models.

Experimental Module

The toolkit includes an optional “Experimental Blind-Test Classifier” that demonstrates pipeline behaviour with pre-trained models. Performance varies depending on the dataset and should not be interpreted as authoritative or publication-ready. This module is included for research exploration only. academic oversight is needed to validate and scrutinise correlations made by TephraLink
